PHOTO­GRAPHS

Drag a picture onto a building, or click to choose one

How this works

Files are saved into photos/ named after the building, downscaled to 1600px if Pillow is installed, and build.py is re-run so the game picks them up. Reload the game to see them.

Only use pictures you have the right to use. Wikimedia Commons and Geograph are both fine with a credit; search results generally are not. If you fetched them with get_photos.py, keep photos/credits.csv beside them and the credits appear in the game automatically.
